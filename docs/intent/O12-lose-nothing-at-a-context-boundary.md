# O12 Lose nothing at a context boundary

Lose nothing at a context boundary: a compaction or a clear costs the task none of its decisions, state or next step.

## Changes

What survives a compaction or a clear, and what the next session has to ask again.

## Known by

After a compaction the next step runs with nothing settled asked again, and nothing done again. The handoff file holds what the summary dropped.

The owner's words, given as the example of an objective and its goals, 2026-10-24. Folded from `session-kit`'s ladder, where it hung from "reduce agent confusion when context grows" and "improve performance".
