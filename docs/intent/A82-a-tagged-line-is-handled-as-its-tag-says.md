# A82 A tagged line is handled as its tag says

The owner may start a line with `Do:`, `Decide:`, `Ask:`, `Later:` or `FYI:`, in English in every session. `Do:` is an order, open until reported. `Decide:` is a binding answer, recorded and not reopened. `Ask:` is a question, answered with no edits and no runs that write. `Later:` is deferred work, written to the plan. `FYI:` is context, with no action. Untagged lines are read as before, and in a tagged message the reply says how it read each one it acted on (`Read as Do: …`). The reply ends with one line per item and the owner's questions last.

## Context

The owner's proposal, `docs/analyses/proposal-prefixes-and-check-ins.md`, with the owner's decisions of 2026-10-29: tags both ways, English only, untagged lines read as today with the reading shown. Mixed messages lost items, and remarks read as orders cost reverts. The read against the kit is `docs/analyses/prefixes-and-check-ins-read.md`.

## Options

- The proposal's draft of about 95 words, with every closing-line form in the rule. Not taken: the forms are needed only on a tagged turn, so the kit gives them there (`A83`), by `A77` (a global rule states its obligation).
- The obligation alone in the rule, the forms in the kit's context. Taken.

## Consequences

The rulebook grows by about 80 words. Where the kit is absent the session still ends with one line per item, without ids. Undone if the owner stops tagging, or if the closing lines cost more reading than the lost items did.
