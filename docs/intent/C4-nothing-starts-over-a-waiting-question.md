# C4 Nothing of the kit's starts a turn or a compaction while a question waits on the owner

A question the session puts to the owner in the app's next-step box waits on the owner until their next prompt. The work responds so: nothing of the kit's starts a turn or a compaction over it, and where something must (the cache about to go cold, the owner's own press), the question goes across whole, with its buttons.

The owner's rule for the defect "the handoff hides a pending question", in `~/devel/galileo/misc/claude-analysis/rules-and-load.md`: "Nothing of the kit's should start a turn or a compaction while a question is waiting for me, and where it has to, the question comes across". Put as a constraint in `docs/analyses/rules-and-load-read.md` and approved on 2026-10-29. The first action held to it is `A90` (the kit waits on a pending question).
