# O17 The system is observed, analysed and improved over time

We observe, analyse and improve this system over time: what the sessions do, and what the hooks and the rest of the kit do to them.

## Changes

What the kit is changed on: evidence from the sessions, in place of impressions.

## Known by

A change to a rule or a hook can be argued from the transcripts and from a measurement of its effect, and the transcripts of past sessions can be read and compared by a tool.

The owner's words, 2026-10-25: "As an objective we need to observe, analyze, and improve this system over time." From `G1`, since the system is the work with agents itself, and from `G4`, since cost and time are what the hooks are measured on.
