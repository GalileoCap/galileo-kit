# C5 Nothing of the load measures reaches a session

The measures of the owner's load (the working window, the hold and its reasons, the focus list, the pass, the weekly review) are the owner's alone. The work responds so: no text of theirs enters a session's context, and they show only on the band, the status line, toasts, the pane and files outside every project.

From the audit `~/devel/galileo/misc/claude-analysis/rules-and-load.md`, part 2a: "the sessions must not find out about any of it". Put as a constraint in `docs/analyses/rules-and-load-read.md` and approved on 2026-10-29.
