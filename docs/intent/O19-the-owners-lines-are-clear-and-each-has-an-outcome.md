# O19 The owner's lines are clear, and each has a visible outcome

Each line the owner sends says plainly what it is for, and the owner can see what became of it: done, left undone with the reason, recorded, parked, or answered. A remark is never taken for an order, and an order is never lost in a mixed message.

Proposed as "for every line the owner sends, the owner can see what became of it" in `docs/analyses/proposal-prefixes-and-check-ins.md`, from a review of the owner's messages across 152 sessions. The owner approved it on 2026-10-29 and widened it: "I approve it, but it's also 'the owner speaks in a clear language' (or something like that)".

## Changes

The owner's messages, which say what each line is, and the agent's replies, which account for each line.

## Known by

The messages of the kind "ask me that question again", "some messages got lost" and "that was only a note", counted per session-hour by the transcript reader: five or more in the live sessions on 2026-10-29, none wanted. Beside it, the share of the turn-end check's prompts on items the reply did address, under one in twenty.
