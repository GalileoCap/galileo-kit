# A3 Port session-kit first

`session-kit` is ported to a mod first and documented through its own intent, and mods are weighed against it after, to fold in or run beside it.

## Context

The owner reordered the work after the first review: a mod we fork may duplicate what the port gives, and the port is the chance to document the kit's intent.

## Consequences

`cache-tax`, reviewed and marked fork, waits on the port's context mod.
