## Open questions

- Mod ordering: does a mod's `tool.call` or `tool.check` deny run before or after another user-installed mod's approval? This decides whether a mod port of the `session-kit` guard can hold against a third-party mod. Source: the "React to events" page, https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/events.md, and the mods admin page on seats (`prepend`, `append`). First evidence, 2026-10-25, right after the install of 0.20.0 in the desktop app: the engine reported that the kit's `prompt.context` hook was skipped because `cc-plugin-sec-default` runs first and sent the event on past it. A mod ahead of the kit can answer an event without calling `next`, so the kit's hooks run at that mod's pleasure, and a guard as a mod would too. Second, during the chain's `/compact` on 0.20.3: the kit's `session.compact` hook, which gives an automatic compaction the handoff's instructions as a backstop, was skipped the same way. Third, the owner's account: the `prompt.context` skip shows on most prompts in the work instance and never in their personal one, so `cc-plugin-sec-default` most likely comes from an organisation policy, seated ahead of every user-installed plugin, where neither the owner nor a plugin can move it. No file of it is on this machine, so it is not read. While it sits there the checkout's handoff advice (`A25`) never reaches the model, and a guard as a mod would hold only where the policy lets it.
- What a compaction or a clear is worth, and how much the reader's sample is biased by the sessions cleared or deleted (questions 9 and 10 of `docs/session-tooling-design.md`). The warning's tiers at 250,000, 300,000 and 400,000 tokens (`A68`, the warning firms at 300,000 tokens and stops at 400,000) were set on the 2026-10-26 reading of lane1, demo-mat-porting and mat-lib, and the measure module records live so the next report sees every session.
