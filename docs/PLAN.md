## Open questions

- Mod ordering: does a mod's `tool.call` or `tool.check` deny run before or after another user-installed mod's approval? This decides whether a mod port of the `session-kit` guard can hold against a third-party mod. Source: the "React to events" page, https://code.claude.com/docs/en/plugins/mods/events.md, and the mods admin page on seats (`prepend`, `append`). First evidence, 2026-10-25, right after the install of 0.20.0 in the desktop app: the engine reported that the kit's `prompt.context` hook was skipped because `cc-plugin-sec-default` runs first and sent the event on past it. A mod ahead of the kit can answer an event without calling `next`, so the kit's hooks run at that mod's pleasure, and a guard as a mod would too. Second, during the chain's `/compact` on 0.20.3: the kit's `session.compact` hook, which gives an automatic compaction the handoff's instructions as a backstop, was skipped the same way.
- What a compaction or a clear is worth, and how much the reader's sample is biased by the sessions cleared or deleted (questions 9 and 10 of `docs/session-tooling-design.md`). The warning's tiers at 250,000, 500,000 and 750,000 tokens are set on the owner's experience, and the measure module records live so the next report sees every session.
- The analysis of the two projects: the order of the tool candidates, whether code-shape rules go in `global/CLAUDE.md` or an opt-in `global/CODE.md`, the rule-ownership conflict with tagents, whether the projects' records converge on the ladder (`docs/analyses/tagents-and-generic-app.md`, last section).
- Login type: is this machine's session credential a personal or a Team/Enterprise login? On a personal login without managed settings, an installed mod can approve a tool call that the `session-kit` `PreToolUse` guard blocks.
